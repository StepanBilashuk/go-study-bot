🎯 Bolt — interview prep

Process: recruiter → live coding → system design → behavioral (hiring manager). ~5 stages, algorithm-heavy, avg ~40 days for senior.

▸ Coding (LeetCode medium, backtracking-heavy live editor)
Grind NeetCode mediums; expect a shared live editor and to talk through your approach.
Study: /learn backtracking · /learn graphs · /learn dynamic-programming · /learn arrays-hashing · /learn trees
Bolt "algorithmic gauntlet" can go deep: /learn advanced-graphs · /learn union-find · /learn greedy

▸ System design (whiteboard / Excalidraw, API-focused)
Real prompts seen: "design a job scheduling service", "design an API".
Study first: /design framework (RESHADED) · /design job-scheduler
Building blocks: /learn api-design · /learn message-queues · /learn distributed-coordination · /learn load-balancing
Bolt domain (mobility): /design ride-sharing · /design food-delivery

▸ Behavioral (ownership, communication)
They explicitly value: communicate clearly, own your mistakes, ask for help when stuck — perfection is not expected.
Prep: /story (ownership, failure) · /stories · /boss behavioral
